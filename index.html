About Our School

Welcome to Sindhu's Vignan Vidyanikethan

Established with a vision to provide quality education, Sindhu's Vignan Vidyanikethan is one of the leading educational institutions in Narasannapeta, Srikakulam district, Andhra Pradesh. We are committed to nurturing young minds with academic excellence and strong moral values.

Our school follows the AP State Board (SSC) curriculum, offering classes from Nursery to Class 10 across two conveniently located branches. We believe in holistic development — combining rigorous academics with co-curricular activities, sports, and character building.
